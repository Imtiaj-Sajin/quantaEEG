\section{Cross-session transfer and the register sweep}

\begin{figure}[htbp]
\centering
\includegraphics[width=\textwidth]{fig10_crosssession_sweep.pdf}
\caption{\label{fig:crosssession}(a) Cross-session transfer within subject on
IV-2a, both directions averaged. In the sensor frame the quantum kernels sit
near chance while the classical baselines do not; whitening each session by
its own label-free mean closes the gap and leaves every SPD-kernel geometry
tied. (b) Off-diagonal Gram variance versus register size in both frames on
PhysioNet ($n=\SweepN$). Solid, reference frame; dashed, sensor frame. In the
reference frame no kernel falls below its three-qubit value at any larger
register.}
